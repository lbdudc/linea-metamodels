# browser

Lists and forms: the tables and the data-entry forms of the product, one of each over each data-model entity, with what a person can do with them and the fields they show. Needs the data model

A metamodel of [LINEA](https://github.com/lbdudc/linea) (`browser` v1.0.0). Its grammars, specs and templates are in this folder; LINEA reads them from its registry (see the [repository README](../../README.md)).
