# featuremodel

Feature model of an SPL (features, groups, constraints) and the feature selection of its products. Its models are level 1 (the SPL's) and level 0 (the products')

A metamodel of [LINEA](https://github.com/lbdudc/linea) (`featuremodel` v1.0.0). Its grammars, specs and templates are in this folder; LINEA reads them from its registry (see the [repository README](../../README.md)).
