# menus

Menus: the navigation of the product, as the platform lays it out: menus of items that open a screen of the platform or group more items

A metamodel of [LINEA](https://github.com/lbdudc/linea) (`menus` v2.0.0). Its grammars, specs and templates are in this folder; LINEA reads them from its registry (see the [repository README](../../README.md)).
